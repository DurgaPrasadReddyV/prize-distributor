Prototype — throwaway, not production

Does the one-prize-per-person rule actually hold?

The question: a person may win exactly one prize, ever. This demo lets you drive the model and watch what happens when two people act at the same time — because that is the case that is hard to be sure about on paper.

Everything here is worked out from facts checked against the real GitHub API: creating a file that already exists is refused, never merged, and two people writing the same new file at once produces one winner and one refusal. What the demo adds is the decision logic — turning those refusals into words a person can act on. Press the buttons in any order; the awkward cases are the point.
